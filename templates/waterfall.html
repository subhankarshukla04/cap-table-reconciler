F Methodology & provenance

  • Liquidation preference cumulative breakpoints — NVCA Model Certificate of Incorporation, Article IV §2 (Liquidation Preference).
  • Conversion-threshold computation — Aranca, "OPM Backsolve worked example for Series B with 1x non-participating preferred"; Plante Moran, "Common stock value at the breakpoint."
  • Participating-with-cap regime transitions — NVCA Model Charter §2.2 (Participation) and Eqvista's "participating preferred with cap" three-regime treatment.
  • Indian CCPS treated as preferred with mandatory conversion at exit — Companies Act 2013, §55; Cyril Amarchand commentary on CCPS waterfall mechanics.
  • Reserved (unallocated) option pool excluded from waterfall per market practice; granted-options pool participates as common.

This page reports breakpoints only. It does not produce a fair-value, an OPM Backsolve, or a 409A. Each breakpoint becomes a separate option strike in any downstream OPM step.
