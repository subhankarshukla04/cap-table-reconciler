## SL04-01 — Series A2 Pay-to-Play Forfeiture + Selective Waiver

**Pay-to-play (Section 4.5(d) of the Restated Articles):** Any holder of preferred stock that does not participate in a Qualifying Subsequent Financing pro-rata to its holdings as of the closing of such financing shall forfeit anti-dilution rights on its existing preferred. The Series A2 round shall be deemed a Qualifying Subsequent Financing.

**Series A2 Side Letter §3 (waiver):** The Company hereby waives the pay-to-play forfeiture provision of Section 4.5(d) with respect to the holder(s) of Series Seed CCPS in consideration of (i) the surrender of board observation rights granted under the original Series Seed Investment Agreement, and (ii) execution of an updated drag-along consent.

### Open scope questions for counsel

1. Does the pay-to-play waiver also preserve any registration rights, or only the AD protection?
2. Is the drag-along consent enforceable against transferees of the Seed shares, or only the original holders?

## SL04-02 — Full-Ratchet Trigger Memo

Memorialises the calculation, board approval, and counsel sign-off for the March 2026 full-ratchet trigger on the Series Seed CCPS.

**Calculation (per NVCA §4.4(c) full-ratchet variant):**

> New Conversion Price = Down-Round Issue Price (full ratchet, by definition).
> Original Seed Conversion Price = ₹50.00.
> Down-Round Issue Price (Series A2) = ₹30.00.
> New Seed Conversion Price = ₹30.00.
> New Conversion Ratio = ₹50.00 / ₹30.00 = 1.6667×.
> Common-equivalent shares: 800,000 × 1.6667 = 1,333,333 (rounded down per charter).
