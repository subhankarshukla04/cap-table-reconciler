# Provenance — Fixture 01 (Clean Baseline)

Every clause in this fixture traces to a published, freely available legal precedent. No client data, no NDA-protected material.

## Company

- **Solstice Labs Pte. Ltd.** — fictional name, fictional Singapore Pte. Ltd. structure for jurisdictional realism. Singapore is Qapita's home jurisdiction; Pte. Ltd. is the standard Singapore private-company suffix.

## Capital structure

- **Founders Common — 4.5M shares.** Vanilla common stock. NVCA Model Certificate of Incorporation, Article IV, Section 1 (common stock authorization). No special rights, no voting class distinction.
- **Option Pool — 2.0M shares (1.5M granted, 0.5M reserved).** Standard 10–15% option pool sizing for a Series B SaaS. Granted-vs-reserved split mirrors industry-standard waterfall practice: granted options (whether vested or not) participate in liquidation proceeds; unallocated reserves do not. Source: AICPA Practice Guide treatment of option pool participation, plus Plante Moran 2015 worked example.

## Round mechanics

All three preferred rounds use the **NVCA Model Certificate of Incorporation** as the master reference, with the standard "single-trigger" 1x non-participating preferred structure:

- **Liquidation preference: 1x non-participating.** NVCA Model Certificate of Incorporation, Article IV, Section 2.2 (Liquidation Preference). The 1x multiple is the modern venture market default for healthy primary rounds; 1.5x and 2x appear in down rounds and distressed financings, neither of which Solstice has experienced.
- **Anti-dilution: broad-based weighted-average.** NVCA Model Certificate of Incorporation, Article IV, Section 2.4(d) (Anti-Dilution Adjustments). The broad-based formula is the modern venture market default. Full ratchet appears in deeply pro-investor or distressed terms; narrow-based weighted-average is a middle ground used selectively. Solstice carries broad-based on every round.
- **Conversion: 1:1 voluntary, mandatory on qualified IPO.** NVCA Model Certificate of Incorporation, Article IV, Section 2.5 (Conversion).
- **No participation.** NVCA Model Certificate of Incorporation, Article IV, Section 2.2(c) — non-participating election. The fixture excludes participation entirely to keep the waterfall pedagogically clean.

## Round sizing

Round amounts (Seed $1M, Series A $5M, Series B $15M) and price-per-share progression ($0.50 → $2.00 → $5.00) reflect benchmarks for a Singapore B2B SaaS company on a standard Seed-A-B trajectory. The 4x and 2.5x step-ups round-to-round are within the typical SEA Series B markup range published in Cento Ventures' annual SEA Tech Investment reports and AVCJ Asia VC market data.
