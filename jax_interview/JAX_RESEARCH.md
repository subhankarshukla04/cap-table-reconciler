## Part 3 — What Founders' Office at a Series B startup actually does

Founders' Office (FO) at this stage is a **chief-of-staff function** wrapped around the CEO. The portfolio of work typically includes:

1. **Board & investor relations** — monthly investor update, quarterly board pack, ad-hoc shareholder asks, cap-table-of-the-house management.
2. **Strategic projects** — anything the CEO needs run that doesn't belong cleanly under Eng / Sales / Ops yet (new geo entry, new product line scoping, partnership origination).
3. **M&A and corp-dev** — sourcing, desktop diligence, deal memos. Qapita's **Punch Financial acquisition (Oct 2025)** is the precedent. Expect more acquisitions in fund-admin / virtual-CFO / 409A-boutique / corp-sec adjacent firms.
4. **Competitive & market intel** — what Carta / Pulley / Hiive / Forge / Globacap / Trica / EquityList shipped this week; what's the SEA fundraising market doing.
5. **Hiring for new lines** — Jax's Indonesia hiring posts fit here. SEA expansion = his hiring funnel.
6. **GTM partnerships** — Schwab is the headline deal of 2025. Future analogues (banks, family-office networks, regional law firms) come through FO.

**The "Financial Analyst, Founders' Office" job description** that Qapita posts on iimjobs and similar boards is consistently about: cap-table modeling, financial modeling and scenario analysis (waterfall / financing-round), market & competitor research, sales-team inputs, platform-development inputs. **In Jax's incarnation in SEA, this lens is regional-flavored** — same job, SEA scope.
